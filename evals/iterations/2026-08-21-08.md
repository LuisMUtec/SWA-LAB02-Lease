## What this round established

The three rounds before this one were about **a figure with no producer**. Iteration 07 found its mirror, **an act with no consumer**. This round found the third member of the family and it is the one that explains the other two: **a repair applied at one altitude and not at the one that governs it.** FR-006b was charged half a point in iteration 07 for barring a refusal — and FR-006b was *written from* a Key Product Decision that said exactly that, which nobody amended. BR-13's exit half was rewritten and its entry half was not. Three of the five D4 findings were defects introduced by this round's own corrections.

The lesson is not "propagate harder". It is that a fix belongs at the **highest** altitude that asserts the wrong thing, and everything below it should then be derived rather than patched.
