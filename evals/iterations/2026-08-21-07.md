## The defect class this round exposed

Iterations 04–06 kept finding **a figure with no producer**. This round found its mirror image: **an act with no consumer.** FR-030 was raised, recorded and retrievable — and reached no one, could not be answered, could not be discharged, and one of its two remedies could not by construction clear the state it existed to clear. Iteration 05 had already found this exact shape once, in FR-030 of `003`, and Julia named it then: a capability nobody was obliged to use. The repair is symmetrical to the producer repair — an act needs a party who can see it, a way to answer it, and a state that its answer actually moves.
